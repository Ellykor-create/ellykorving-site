My story

25 years in business.
Living through crises.
And still standing here.

For years I lived the way many people live. Pushing on. Carrying responsibility. Always switched on.

I ran a family business, worked fifty and sometimes sixty hours a week and kept all the balls in the air. From the outside it looked like everything was right. But somewhere along the way I drifted further and further from myself.

My nervous system was constantly on. I barely knew rest anymore. I lived on autopilot.

I had built my life around my work, instead of my work around my life.

Until life brought me to a halt.

And it was in that stillness that something happened I never expected. I didn't find something new. I remembered who I had always been, beneath all the roles, expectations and patterns.

That moment changed everything.

Not because my outer world changed straight away, but because I began to see myself differently. I started to notice how many people live from adaptation, control and survival, without truly feeling what is right.

In that search I began to learn from the greatest thinkers in consciousness, transformation and human potential. From Tony Robbins to Eckhart Tolle. From Dr. Joe Dispenza to Michael Singer, Bob Proctor and Kim Calvert.

Not as a follower. But as someone who wanted to understand it all, live it through and translate it into real life.

I didn't simply absorb knowledge. I tested it. Lived it. Lost myself in it and found myself again.

Over the years this grew into my own way of seeing. A blend of awareness, identity, resonance, the nervous system and entrepreneurship.

Ever since, that has been the foundation of my work.

I help people slow down, so they can hear themselves again. Settle their nervous system. Make clear choices. Live and work from who they truly are.

And perhaps the most beautiful part of all: the work that once felt heavy no longer even feels like work.

Because it fits.

Not harder. Not faster.
But more real.

Elly Korving is a Dutch identity coach, life coach and business coach. She works under the name Harmonic Mirror and guides entrepreneurs, leaders and professionals in recognising the patterns, conditioning and self-image from which they live and do business.

Elly Korving is the founder of 110 Life & Business Awareness, a platform for conscious business, identity work and spiritual leadership. Together with Alterego Branding & Strategy she builds brands from the inside out, identity first, then strategy.

Her methods include the SZINN Alignment Method, 365 Gates of Awareness (daily awareness questions), Entering Transformation, The Awareness Reset and Being the Laws of My Universe.

Elly Korving works with themes such as identity and self-remembering, pattern recognition, breaking conditioning, conscious choice, spiritual leadership, conscious business, inner growth, resonance and alignment.
