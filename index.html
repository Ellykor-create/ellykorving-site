The starting point
From the inside out

What if there is more to you than you’ve come to believe?

Throughout life, you form beliefs about who you are, what you can do, and what is possible for you. Some still fit. Others may be ready to change.

I guide you to recognise these patterns, explore what lies beyond them, and remember what feels true to you. With greater awareness comes room to make different choices, in your life, your work, and the way you move forward.

Harmonic Mirror

Mirror. Guide. Bridge.

Three roles. One movement. Starting from who you are.

01Mirror+

I don’t tell you who to be. We look at the patterns and beliefs shaping your choices, so you can recognise what is truly yours and what you have learned.

02Resonance+

Explore where your choices, work and relationships feel in line with you, and where something asks for a different direction.

03Awareness+

Notice how you perceive, react and choose. Awareness creates room to respond more consciously.

04Stillness+

Pause the noise of busyness and expectations, and create space to hear yourself again.

05Imagination+

Begin with a different image of yourself. Explore who you can be before that possibility becomes visible in your life.

The person behind the work

25 years in business.
Human first.

I know what it is to keep going. To carry responsibility. To build a life around work, until you begin to wonder where you went.

My own experience of entrepreneurship and change shaped the way I work today. I bring together identity, awareness and business to help you see yourself and your choices more clearly.

More of my story +

For years I ran a family business, worked fifty and sometimes sixty hours a week, and kept all the balls in the air. I had built my life around my work, instead of my work around my life.

Until life brought me to a halt. In that stillness, I began to see myself differently, beneath the roles, expectations and patterns. Over the years, this grew into my own way of seeing: a blend of awareness, identity, resonance and entrepreneurship.

Today, that experience is the foundation of my work. Not harder. Not faster. But more real.
